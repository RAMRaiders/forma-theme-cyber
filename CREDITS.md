## Best sellers' cartridges and discs

The pictures in `public/systems/<system>/bestsellers/` are photos and scans of the real
cartridges and discs, from the [LaunchBox Games Database](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/),
contributed by its community. The games, their label art and their names belong to their
publishers; they are shown here to identify each system's best sellers. The European release
is used where the database has one (not for the Atari 2600, whose European releases were
often retitled copies).
Where it has no photo of the real one, a fan-made picture of it is used (marked Fanart).
