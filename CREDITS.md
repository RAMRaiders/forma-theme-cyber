## Best sellers' and classics' cartridges, discs and tapes

The pictures in `public/systems/<system>/bestsellers/` are photos and scans of the real
cartridges and discs, from the [LaunchBox Games Database](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/),
contributed by its community. The games, their label art and their names belong to their
publishers; they are shown here to identify each system's best sellers. The European release
is used where the database has one (the North American one for American systems such as
Atari, Coleco and Intellivision, whose European releases were often retitled copies). Where it
has no photo of the real one, a fan-made picture of it is used (marked Fanart).

A system with no known sales figures shows Classics instead: the games most rated on the
LaunchBox Games Database that have a picture of their real media (compilations and level packs
left out), or picked by hand where those ratings say little.
